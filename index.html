🛸 UFO ABDUCTION

Beam up cows, cars and people before time runs out. Every catch adds time.
Then fly up to space and explore 14 worlds: Las Vegas, Egypt, the Moon, Mars and more.

📋 Controls & scoring
Left stick – fly forward & turn
▲ ▼ – go up / down
BEAM – hold over a cow or car
BOOST – fly fast (uses power)
FIRE – shoot lasers at helicopters, jets & missiles
🎯 Missions – 3 goals on the left side of the screen; finish them for Hangar credits
🛬 LAND – fly low and tap LAND to walk around as your alien (▲ jumps, tap twice to double-jump). Find 5 💠 relics on each world for an 🏅 Explorer Badge!
Power-ups – fly through glowing icons: 🧲 🛡️ ✖️2 ⏱️ 🔥 ⚡
Tip: the beam only reaches when you're below 70m.

A / D – turn
R / F – go up / down
SPACE – tractor beam · SHIFT – boost · X – fire
L – land and get out (fly low first) · L again to get back in
