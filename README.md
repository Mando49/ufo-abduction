# 🛸 UFO Abduction

A 3D browser game: fly a triangle UFO over a neon cyberpunk city and farmland, and use your tractor beam to abduct cows and cars before time runs out.

**Play:** https://mando49.github.io/ufo-abduction/

🌊 **The Ocean:** take the 🌊 portal in Earth Orbit to dive into an underwater world — coral reefs, swaying kelp forests, sunbeams, bubbles, fish schools, stinging jellyfish, and fish (15), sea turtles (25), sharks (40) and whales (150) to beam up. A sunken pirate galleon is hidden somewhere. Fly up through the surface to return to space.

🐙 **Ocean upgrade:** the **Kraken** sleeps in a dark pit in the ocean — get close and it wakes, its tentacles reach for you and can grab the ship (hold BOOST to break free). Zap its head with FIRE to defeat it (+500, drops treasure; it comes back after a minute). Also: 7 glowing **sunken treasure chests** (fly low to open them for points + credits), 3 **ocean currents** that carry you fast like a water slide, **manta rays** gliding overhead, and a glowing **plankton trail** behind the ship. New missions: Defeat the Kraken, Open 5 chests, Ride a current.

❄️ **Frostara upgrade:** falling snow, the **Frost Palace** (crystal towers, ice walls, a giant ice gate to fly through), a **frozen lake** with an ancient ice dragon trapped below (its eye glows when you get close), an **igloo village** with waddling **penguins** to abduct, and **Yetis** that throw snowballs at your ship (a hit frosts your screen and slows you) — beam one up for +150. Less glare on the snow. New missions: Abduct a Yeti, Abduct 5 penguins, Fly through the Frost Palace gate.

🏁 **Sky Race:** fly up above the clouds over the city to the checkered 🏁 SKY RACE ring. Touch 20 rings in order — each one makes the ship faster (up to x3 speed). Halfway around, dive into the 🕳️ black hole shortcut to skip ahead at max speed. Follow the floating arrow, beat your best time, and earn bonus score + credits.

🕳️ **The Black Hole:** in Earth Orbit, fly toward the giant black hole (past Mars). Its gravity pulls you in — boost away, or let it take you: the ship spirals and stretches, the screen goes dark, you ride through the inside, and get spat out on a random planet or town (+250).

✨ **The 5th Dimension:** a secret world you can only reach through the black hole (about a 40% chance). Neon grid floor, real 4D tesseracts spinning through the 4th dimension, upside-down floating islands, melting-time clocks, color-shifting sky, and glowing cube beings to abduct.

🎯 **Missions:** 3 goals are always on screen (abduct police cars, beat the Mothership, land on Mars, Ring Run Saturn, find a secret, climb a pyramid… 30 in all). Finish one for Hangar credits and a new one takes its place. Progress is saved between games; tap the panel to hide it.

📱 **Install it like an app:** open the link in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen* (Android: Chrome menu → *Install app*). It opens full screen with its own icon.

🌌 **Two ways to play:** *Arcade* (2:30 on the clock, beat your best score) or *Free Explore* (no timer, your shield reboots instead of game over; tap ⏹ to end and bank your credits).

## How to play
- **Phone:** left stick to fly and turn · ▲▼ to go up/down · hold **BEAM** over a cow or car · **BOOST** to fly fast
- **Keyboard:** W/S fly · A/D turn · R/F up/down · Space beam · Shift boost
- 🐄 Cow = 10 pts (+4s) · 🚗 Car = 25 pts (+6s) · fly through the neon ring for +15 pts (+5s)
- 🚚 Army truck (Area 51) = 40 pts · 💎 Space crystal = 5 pts
- The beam only reaches when you're below 70m. Don't crash into the skyscrapers!
- ⭐ Abductions raise your wanted level: helicopters (beam them up from above for 60 pts!), then fighter jets with homing missiles. Boost to outrun missiles; your shield recharges when you stop taking hits.
- 🛠️ Every point you score goes into your bank. Spend it in the **Hangar** on upgrades (engine, beam, armor, power) and new ships (Classic Saucer, Shadow Triangle, Mothership).
- ✨ **Power-ups** float around every area (diamonds on the radar), each lasting 12s: 🧲 Magnet Beam (huge beam), 🛡️ Shield Bubble (no damage), ✖️2 Double Points, ⏱️ Slow-Mo (enemies slowed), 🔥 Rapid Fire (fast, free lasers), ⚡ Turbo (unlimited power, faster boost).
- 🔫 **FIRE** shoots auto-aiming lasers: blast helicopters (50), jets (80) and even incoming missiles.
- ☁️ Climb above the clouds into the **Sky Realm**: a clean sea of white clouds under a sunset sky.
- 🚀 Climb past 360m to reach space, then fly through a portal to travel between **Neon City** and the **Area 51 desert**. Space is wide open now — fly anywhere, and land on 🌕 **the Moon** (craters, a moon base, lander, Earth in the sky, lunar rovers to abduct) or 🔴 **Mars** (red canyons, dust devils, a dome colony, little green Martians). Earth Orbit is a mini solar system: 🪐 Saturn (fly through its rings for +50 and +10s), Jupiter with its moons, and ☀️ the Sun (get too close and it burns your shield). Rounds start at 2:30 and ⏰ Bonus Time pickups add +20s. Deep-space wormholes link four sectors (Earth Orbit → Frost Sector → Inferno Sector → Jungle Nebula), each with a planet you can land on and explore: ❄️ **Frostara** (ice spikes, crystals, aurora, snow critters), 🔥 **Magmor** (lava rivers, volcanoes, lava crabs) and 🍄 **Verdana** (giant glowing mushrooms, blob aliens). Alien critters are worth 20. Portals are swirling wormholes you ride through (grab energy orbs on the way). Space is a shooter: alien hunter ships attack in waves, asteroids can be blasted for crystals, an **Alien Mothership boss** shows up after 8 kills (+500), fly through the space station ring for a power refill, and there's a black hole and comets in the distance.

3D models for the cows, cars, police cars, army trucks, helicopters, jets and the Shadow Triangle ship were made with AI (Higgsfield / Tripo) and are in `assets/models/`.

🔺 **Pyramid Valley** in the Area 51 desert (three climbable pyramids, an alien sphinx, obelisks and an oasis) plus alien pyramids on Mars.

🛬 **Alien Explorer:** fly low and tap LAND to step out as your little green alien. Walk, run and double-jump around any world (lower gravity on the Moon and Mars!). Each world hides 5 💠 relics (purple dots on the radar); find all 5 for that world's 🏅 Explorer Badge (7 to collect, saved on your device). The timer pauses while you're on foot. Hold JUMP in the air to fly with the **jetpack** (uses power, refills on the ground), and tap **👀 ZAP** to fire laser eyes — blast the ❓ mystery crates (orange on the radar) for points, bonus time, power or credits. Tap 🛸 SHIP to fly again. Every world also has a 🔍 hidden secret to discover on foot (crashed saucer, secret bunker, crystal cave, lava temple, blob village, mystery monolith, ancient ruins) worth +200 and +500 bonus credits the first time. Track everything in the Hangar's Explorer Log.

🎵 **Music:** a synthwave soundtrack generated live in the browser, with its own song for every area (Neon City, Area 51, the clouds, space, wormholes, boss fights/high wanted level, Frostara, Magmor, Verdana, the Moon and Mars). Toggle with the 🎵 button.

🎬 **Promo Camera:** tap 🎬 (or PROMO CAMERA on the menu) to hide the controls and let the ship fly itself while the camera cuts between cinematic shots — perfect for screen-recording TikTok / Reels clips. Tap anywhere to exit.

Built with three.js. No install needed: it's a single `index.html` file.
